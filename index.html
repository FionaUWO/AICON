About Us

The AICON Lab, led by Dr. Fang (Fiona) Fang, is affiliated with the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering and the Department of Computer Science at Western University. AICON develops AI-native and quantum-enabled connectivity, optimization, and networking technologies that embed intelligence directly into communication, computing, and network infrastructure.

Our research advances an AI–network co-evolution paradigm in which AI autonomously optimizes network operation, resource orchestration, and system performance, while connectivity infrastructure operationalizes and scales distributed intelligence, including edge and federated learning. We also develop resource-efficient and scalable quantum optimization and machine-learning methods for complex problems in wireless communications, networking, and other industry applications.

Our work emphasizes deployable solutions, including intelligent radio environments such as RIS-enabled systems, AI- and quantum-driven system optimization, integrated communication–sensing–computing, and wireless edge intelligence. Through experimental testbeds, system prototyping, quantum-hardware validation, and industry collaboration, AICON translates these innovations into practical technologies for 6G networks, smart infrastructure, autonomous systems, and mission-critical industrial applications.
